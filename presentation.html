How Do We Know Customers Are Happy? Karur Vysya Bank · Customer Experience & Loyalty Training

  • If we provide good service today, how do we know customers are satisfied?
  • How do we measure their experience?
  • How do we know they will stay with Karur Vysya Bank?

Answer: Organizations use customer metrics to measure sentiment and loyalty.

  • 📊 CSAT — Satisfaction with today's interaction
  • ⚡ CES — Effort required to get work done
  • ❤️ NPS — Loyalty & willingness to recommend

Why Measure Customer Experience?

"What gets measured gets improved."

📌 Customer measurements help us:

  • Understand customer expectations
  • Identify pain points
  • Improve service delivery
  • Increase loyalty
  • Strengthen Karur Vysya Bank's reputation

💡 Key Message

  • Assumptions cannot improve service.
  • Customer feedback can.
  • Metrics act as a health check-up for customer experience.

CSAT Customer Satisfaction Score

📖 Definition

  • Measures how satisfied a customer is with a specific interaction at Karur Vysya Bank.
  • Question: "How satisfied were you today?"
  • Rating: 1 to 5 or 1 to 10
  • Measures immediate satisfaction.

🤔 Audience Poll

  • "Can a customer be satisfied today but leave Karur Vysya Bank tomorrow?"
  • Answer: Yes
  • Satisfaction alone does not guarantee loyalty.

CES Customer Effort Score

📖 Definition & Question

  • Measures how easy or difficult it was to get work done at Karur Vysya Bank.
  • Question: "How easy was it to complete your request?"
  • Customers prefer easy experiences.

⚖️ Effort Comparison

  • Low Effort: Single visit · Quick info · No follow-ups
  • High Effort: Multiple visits · Repeated documents · Repeated follow-ups

Key Message: Customers remember the effort they invested more than the effort Karur Vysya Bank invested.

NPS Net Promoter Score

📖 Definition

  • Measures customer loyalty toward Karur Vysya Bank.
  • Question: "How likely are you to recommend Karur Vysya Bank to friends or family?"
  • Scale: 0 to 10

💡 Why Important?

  • Recommendation reflects trust.
  • Satisfaction measures today's experience.
  • NPS measures future loyalty.

Promoters, Passives & Detractors

😍 Promoters (9–10)

  • Loyal to Karur Vysya Bank
  • Actively recommend
  • Positive word-of-mouth

😐 Passives (7–8)

  • Satisfied but not loyal
  • Not emotionally connected
  • May switch to a competitor

😠 Detractors (0–6)

  • Unhappy with Karur Vysya Bank
  • May complain publicly
  • May discourage others
  • Objective: Convert passives → promoters, reduce detractors

Understanding NPS Calculation

🧮 Formula

  • NPS = % Promoters − % Detractors
  • Example: 60% Promoters · 25% Passives · 15% Detractors

📈 Result & Interpretation

  • NPS = +45
  • Higher score = stronger customer loyalty
  • Karur Vysya Bank's goal is to keep raising this number

Three Customer Experience Outcomes

After every interaction at Karur Vysya Bank, one of three outcomes occurs:

1. Mismatch Effect

  • Service delivered < Customer expectation
  • Result: Dissatisfaction, complaints

2. Parity Effect

  • Service delivered = Customer expectation
  • Result: Satisfaction — but not impressed

3. Exceed Effect

  • Service delivered > Customer expectation
  • Result: Delight, appreciation, strong loyalty
  • Creates promoters for Karur Vysya Bank

Customer Journey Through Outcomes

  • Mismatch → Complaint
  • Parity → Satisfaction
  • Exceed → Loyalty → Advocacy

Key Message: The goal is not merely to satisfy customers. The goal is to create promoters of Karur Vysya Bank.
